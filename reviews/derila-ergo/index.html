The Science: Vagus Nerve Support

The vagus nerve is the longest cranial nerve in your body, running from your brainstem through your neck and into your abdomen. It's the master controller of your parasympathetic nervous system - the "rest and digest" mode that's essential for quality sleep.

When your neck is positioned incorrectly during sleep, it can compress or strain the vagus nerve, keeping your body in a low-level "fight or flight" state. This is why many people wake up feeling tired despite getting 7-8 hours of sleep.
